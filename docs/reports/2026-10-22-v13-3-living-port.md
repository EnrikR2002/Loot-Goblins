# V13.3: The Living Port (2026-10-22)

Ninety's brief: a playtester enjoyed the raids but was not moved to keep playing past a round or two, so give goblins reasons to play another five minutes, another raid and another day, through active novelty, progression, social competition and collecting, without chores. Built by Claude Opus 5.5 on branch `feature/v13-3-living-port`, from `main` at `859f35e` (V13.2, Sea of Chaos, merged through PRs #24 to #27). **The PR is not merged**, as the brief asked.

**Read this first.** As the brief asked, **nobody played V13.3 to build it**: no AI playthroughs, no screenshots, no simulated controls, no debug hooks. It was validated by code review, StyLua and Selene, both Rojo builds, **136 automated Luau tests** run in Studio's Edit mode, and **one start of Play to read the console** (empty of errors on server and client), stopped at once. Every game, panel and board below is therefore **unplayed**: whether it works in the hands of a person, reads at a glance and is fun is the team's checklist in section 9. The full description is `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`, **V13.3: The Living Port**.

## 2. How it fits the game

- **Isolation from raids.** The raid always wins: `Raid.onDeparting` ends a goblin's Port game before they are sent (and a goblin signed up is warned 15 s before), a goblin whose `Zone` becomes `raid` or who starts watching leaves their game, Port boats (`Boats.spawnPortBoat`) and Port launchers are left alone by the raid's resets, and every venue is inside the Port's zone. Nothing in V13.3 runs during a raid except quest and leaderboard bookkeeping on the server's own raid events.
- **Server authority.** Scores, times, hits and catches are the server's measurements; moving targets are functions of the server clock shared with the clients (`PortGames`), and hits are judged analytically (`PortGames.shotHit`), so nothing the client says about a hit is believed. Inputs are rate-limited and clamped. Every Shiny is paid by `Progress` with its cap in the profile. Known gaps (the Regatta tub and a running goblin move on their own clients) are written in GAME_DESIGN.md, "Who decides what".
- **Per-frame work.** One Heartbeat (`Minigames.tick`) only for running sessions; the games tick their own simulations at 10 to 20 Hz (crabs, the Derby, Regatta checkpoints); publishing is change-only and at most four times a second; clients draw venues within 320 studs and isle pets within 240, with `BulkMoveTo`.
- **Input.** Every game's action has a keyboard key, a gamepad button and a touch button (ContextActionService), and the panels are V11's `Ui` kit (mouse, touch, gamepad selection). None of it has been tried on a phone or a controller.
- **Robux.** Nothing new is sold for Robux and no product id was invented; everything is Shinies or earned.

## 3. Economy (summary)

Raids stay the main earner: a typical raid pays about 24 Shinies in 7.3 minutes (unchanged). Expected per day: **casual ~155 (raids ~62%), active ~510 (~66%), skilled ~630 (~63%)**. Caps: Port games 40 a day (6 paid runs per game, 3 best bonuses), daily quests ~55, weekly quests 50 to 100 each, expeditions 30, challenges 5 paid wins (2 per opponent, forfeits pay nothing), leaderboards once per period. Restarts, rejoining and switching servers cannot farm (caps are in the profile); alternate accounts earn only their own capped amounts and cannot pass them on (no trading). Full table: GAME_DESIGN.md, "The reward budget".

## 4. Persistence and migration

Profile version 4 (`ProfileSchema.VERSION`). A v2 or v3 profile upgrades keeping every item, Shiny, stat and achievement, with empty V13.3 tables; damaged V13.3 tables are repaired; a newer version is never opened or written (unchanged rule). Earnings made while a profile was locked by another server merge across once, V13.3 counters included. Season rewards are marked paid in the same profile change that pays them; expeditions are removed in the same change that pays them; the Hall's final record is written with an `UpdateAsync` that never replaces a final one. Tests: `PersistenceSpec` (v3 → v4, damaged v4), `SeasonsSpec` (paid once, trimmed only after paid), `HabitatSpec` (paid once, daily cap).

## 5. Retention instrumentation

Milestones once per goblin ever: `FirstRaidCompleted`, `SecondRaidStarted`, `SecondRaidCompleted`, `FirstMinigamePlayed`, `FirstQuestCompleted`, `FirstExpeditionCollected`, `FirstSpecialtyBoat`. A `RaidContinuation` funnel per play session (raid finished → next raid joined → next raid finished): **its step 1 → 2 conversion is the brief's most important question**, whether a goblin who finishes a raid chooses another (raids are opt-in since V13.2, so it is always a choice). Also `RaidJoined`, `SessionSummary`, `ReturnSession`, `PortGamePlayed`, `QuestClaimed`, `ChallengePlayed`, and economy SKUs per source. How to read them in the Creator Hub: GAME_DESIGN.md, "Retention instrumentation". **There is no data yet, and nothing here claims V13.3 improves retention.**

## 7. Bugs found and fixed before anyone played

- **The pet isles' settings replaced V9's sanctuary.** They were first named `Config.SANCTUARY`, which already held the safe water round Goblin Cove (`RADIUS`, `BUOYS`): pursuits, the sea-run buoys, the map and the minimap would have errored in every raid. `ChaosSpec` caught it; the isles' table is now `Config.PET_ISLES`.
- **The specialty-boat milestone was never sent**: now sent on buying a boat that is not free and after any Robux grant that brings one.
- Found while building, by review and the new tests: the quest dealer could deal the same quest two days running (now a per-cycle shuffle); a quest reset inside a claim could lose the week's cosmetic (the reset's payout is now settled wherever it happens); a 32-bit hash overflowed Luau's exact integers (split multiplication); waiting challenge sessions counted as running heats and could block a venue; the Regatta's reef could be ridden over; a client drew range targets rotated wrongly; the golf tee's cannon would have answered the skyship's controls; a challenge record that never started was never forgotten.

## 9. Human playtest checklist (three people, about 45 minutes)

Start: double-click **Play Loot Goblins**, then in Studio **Test > Server & Clients > 3 players > Play**. In the Port press `H` to see what is where. Saving does not persist in Studio (it resets when Play stops), so test saving and the weekly reset only in a published test place with **Enable Studio Access to API Services** on.

1. **First look (1 min each).** Without the help card, can you find the seven games, the Quest Board and the pink balloon lift? Do the signs say what each game is?
2. **Every game once** (each player picks two or three; do them together where you can):
   - **Regatta**: all three race. Is a lap about 30 to 45 s? Does anyone dare the gap in the Teeth? Is being thrown by a splash cannon funny or unfair? Does RESET (`Backspace`) put you back sensibly?
   - **Gauntlet**: can everyone finish? Is falling back to the last platform fair? Too hard or too easy?
   - **Cannon Golf**: can you reach the far green? Is hold-to-charge clear? Do your landings score where they look like they should?
   - **Pet Derby**: does cheering feel like timing, not luck? Does it make you laugh?
   - **Treasure Toss** and **Target Gallery**: do hits count when they look like hits? Is the King's mouth readable?
   - **Crab Chaos**: can the three of you catch every crab (the team bonus)? Is chasing crabs fun or annoying?
3. **Leaving.** In any game: press LEAVE; walk away during the lobby; join the raid while playing (the game should warn you 15 s before the raid leaves, then end); reset your character. Nothing should get stuck (a frozen goblin, a game that never ends, a sign stuck on "busy").
4. **Quests.** Open the board. Is every quest clear? Do one daily quest from a Port game and one from a raid, and claim them. Did a finished quest show on screen when it finished?
5. **After a raid.** Play a raid, come home: does the what's-next card show something you actually wanted to do next? Was it in the way? Did you want to play another raid?
6. **Challenges.** Challenge each other from the ⚔ chip: say no once, let one expire, accept one, leave halfway through one (the other should be told they won), and press REMATCH after one.
7. **Sanctuary.** Ride the lift. Are your pets there? Feed them, throw the ball, ring the bell. Visit each other's isles. Buy a cheap decoration and place it. Send a pet on the Shore Scavenge (2 h); in a published place, come back later and collect it.
8. **Leaderboards.** After a Regatta, a golf round and a raid with gold, do your scores appear on the Hall of Records within about two minutes? Are friends marked?
9. **Feel.** Is the Port lively or a wall of menus? Is anything on screen too wordy? Any stutter with three goblins and their pets out?
10. **If you can**: a phone or a controller in one game each.

**How to report**: say what felt bad or confusing, and send a screenshot of any red error (View > Output). Numbers you think are wrong (a medal time, a price, a reward) are easy to change; name the game and the number.

## 10. Not tested, risks and first guesses

- **Every number is a first guess**, the course times above all: medal times for the Regatta (36 / 43 / 54 s) and the Gauntlet (40 / 55 / 80 s) come from distances and speeds, not from anyone racing. The Gauntlet's gaps were checked geometrically (`PortGamesSpec`), not by a goblin jumping them.
- **Live DataStores** (boards, the Hall, season payments) ran only against in-memory stand-ins. Their request budgets were sized for a full server (a board read every 2 min, a write per board per goblin at most every 20 s) but never measured.
- **Two or more players**: heats, queues, challenges, visiting, ties and forfeits were exercised only by the code's own rules and tests.
- **Client-moved things**: a modified client could drive a Regatta tub faster or cut Gauntlet corners within limits (both written down as known gaps).
- **Isles** are rebuilt whenever their look changes; with 32 goblins decorating at once that is a burst of parts. More than 32 goblins: the extras get no isle.
