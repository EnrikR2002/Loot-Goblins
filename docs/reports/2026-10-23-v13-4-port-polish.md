# V13.4: The Living Port, polished (2026-10-23)

Ninety's brief: V13.3 laid the foundations (seven Port games, quests, leaderboards, pet isles, challenges, the after-raid card); make that content **feel finished, inviting and worth returning to**, and make **raid leaderboards the centre of competitive progression**. No new major system, no new game. Built by Claude Opus 5.5 on branch `feature/v13-4-port-polish`, stacked on `feature/v13-3-living-port` (V13.3's PR #28 was open and unmerged when this started, so V13.4 builds on it). **Not merged**, as the brief asked.

**Read this first.** As the brief asked, **nobody played V13.4 to build it**: no AI playthroughs, no screenshots, no simulated input. It was checked by code review, StyLua, Selene, both Rojo builds, **140 automated Luau tests** in Studio's Edit mode, and **one start of Play to read the console** (no errors), stopped at once. Whether it feels better is the team's checklist in section 8. Full description: `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`, **V13.4: The Living Port, polished**.

## 1. Raid leaderboards (the priority)

Audited first: V13.3's boards worked (server-measured scores, per-period OrderedDataStores, a durable Hall of final standings, rewards paid once into the profile) but stood in a row of five equal boards behind the podium, with places that only updated every two minutes and nothing on screen pointing at them.

| What | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly **BIGGEST HAUL** | Kept as is (one raid's banked gold). |
| Weekly **PIRATE CHAMPIONSHIP** | **New** board `crown`: a goblin's best 3 raids of the week added up (bounded, so grinding never wins by itself). Tiers 25 / 50 / 80. #1 **Captain of the Week** title; top 10 Leaderboard Pennant + **Champion's Standard** flag; gold tier **Lucky Doubloon** pet hat. |
| Monthly **PIRATE LEGENDS** | V13.3's monthly Pirate Championship **renamed, id `champion` kept**: its DataStore, Hall records, saved bests and unpaid rewards carry over untouched. Best 5 raids. Adds **Legend's Gilt** sails at its gold tier. |
| Port records | Regatta, Golf, Gauntlet boards kept, shown second and smaller. |

- **Places show at once**: a new best is placed among the board's last read (`Seasons.rankAmong`), so the after-raid card, the boards and the 🏆 chip show it straight away.
- **The Hall of Legends** (rebuilt where the Hall of Records stood, behind the podium): the three raid boards in the middle on gilded pillars (time left, top eight, best goblin in this Port, your line and place, what #1 wins), **PAST CHAMPIONS** (last week's and month's top three), PORT RECORDS on the side, a lit golden trophy over the sign, braziers. Climbing into a raid board's **top three** puts a line in everyone's feed and fireworks over the Hall.
- **On screen**: a **🏆 chip** on the Port screen (your best raid place) opens the new **🏆 LEGENDS** page: a card per raid board with your place, score, a bar to the next tier, time left and prizes.
- **After a raid**: the what's-next card shows each raid board this raid moved ("💰 #4 BIGGEST HAUL ▲5", "NEW!"), and **⚔ RAID AGAIN** is the first, gold, pulsing button.
- **Data**: `profile.season.week` (the week's best raids) beside `season.raids`; a V13.3 profile is repaired to an empty week; busy-profile merges cover both. Real OrderedDataStore behaviour is **still unverified** until a published-place test (Studio used in-memory stand-ins).

## 2. The games

- **All seven**: ▶ **START NOW** in a lobby (everyone in it presses: it starts at once); your **best to beat** in the panel title; countdown ticks and a GO flash; a ★ NEW BEST flash when a points game passes your best; a results card that says **how close** ("★ NEW BEST! −1.34 s", "+2.10 s off your best", "6 short of your best") and the **next medal** to chase; **`R`** (gamepad `Y`) plays again.
- **Regatta**: a **rocket start** (throttle right on GO: a 1.1 s boost; held through the countdown is a wheelspin and nothing); **splits** at every checkpoint against your best run this visit; "▲ 2ND" when you overtake.
- **Gauntlet**: **shortcuts count** (landing on a later platform credits the ones skipped, ⚡ SHORTCUT!); splits like the Regatta.
- **Pet Derby**: a **timing bar** (red too soon, green clean, gold perfect); a **perfect** cheer is a longer burst and a **somersault** over the hurdle; three clean in a row is **ON FIRE** (🔥, flames, a faster burst) until a tumble or mashing; a **commentator** calls new leaders, tumbles, fire, the final stretch, photo finishes and the winner. Every pet still runs the same speed; bots are never perfect; nothing is bought.
- **Treasure Toss and Target Gallery**: **streaks** (from the third hit in a row, +1 a hit; a miss ends it).
- **Crab Chaos**: crabs left on the panel (the team bonus goal), 3 / 2 / LAST CRAB! callouts.
- **Cannon Golf**: only the shared improvements above.

## 3. The Port, quests, challenges, sanctuary

- **Wayfinding**: a colour-coded **signpost** on the plaza (an arm for every game, the Hall, the Quest Board, the pet isles' lift, the raids), and over each venue a **tall flag in its game's colour** with its icon. Decoration only; nothing solid.
- **Quests**: **CLAIM ALL**; a "+18 ◆" pop on every claim; less small print.
- **Challenges**: the result shows **both scores**; a tie is a DEAD HEAT; when the other asks for a **rematch** first, an ACCEPT card appears for its 15 s.
- **Sanctuary**: your pets **run to greet you** when you step onto your isle. Passive income unchanged.

## 4. Economy and anti-exploit

Raids stay the main source. New: the weekly Championship's tiers and places (the existing weekly amounts: 10–35 tier, 15–100 place), its cosmetics, and the range streak point inside the existing daily caps (6 paid runs a game, 40 Shinies a day). Checked by design and tests: the week's raid list is bounded and saved in the profile (rejoining and server hops change nothing); season rewards still paid once in the same profile change; the rocket start is accepted once per race and only within 0.6 s of GO; a shortcut must still reach the platforms and beat `MIN_TIME`; forfeits still pay nothing; expeditions unchanged. Known gap (as V13.3): the Regatta tub and Gauntlet runner move on their own client, so a modified client could still drive faster within limits; the rocket start trusts the client's timing within the server's window in the same way.

## 5. Bugs found and fixed before anyone played

- **The new top-ten flag reused an existing id**: V13.4's "Crown Banner" was first `flag_crown`, the id of the sold Golden Crown Banner (its look would have been silently replaced and the catalogue would hold two items with one id). `CatalogSpec` caught it; it is now `flag_champion`, the **Champion's Standard** (crimson, three gold stars).
- A flag's stars first used a ball part, which Roblox squashes to its thinnest side (they would have been invisible): now egg meshes, like the other flags' emblems.

## 7. Known limitations

- **Unplayed and unlooked-at**: the Hall's layout (its left end reaches onto the edge of the Shipyard's quay), the signpost arms and venue flags (placed by numbers), the timing bar, the LEGENDS page at small screen sizes.
- **First guesses**: Championship tiers (25 / 50 / 80), rocket start (0.25 s window, 1.1 s boost), the Derby's perfect window (0.28 s before to 0.08 s after) and ON FIRE (1.7×), the streak bonus.
- An instant place is an estimate against the board's last read (up to two minutes old); the next read corrects it. Splits are kept only for the current visit.

## 8. Human playtest checklist (three people, about 30 minutes)

Start: double-click **Play Loot Goblins**, then **Test > Server & Clients > 3 players > Play**.

1. **Find the Hall of Legends** from the plaza without help (the signpost and the trophy). Can you read the boards? Do you see PAST CHAMPIONS and the PORT RECORDS?
2. **Raid twice** with gold. After each: does the what's-next card show your places ("#4 ▲")? Did RAID AGAIN tempt you? Check the 🏆 chip and the LEGENDS page.
3. **Regatta**: press START NOW; try the rocket start (W right on GO, then too early). Race twice: do the green/red splits make you want a third go? Does `R` restart quickly?
4. **Gauntlet**: look for a shortcut. Does ⚡ SHORTCUT! ever show?
5. **Pet Derby**: is the timing bar readable? Does PERFECT feel earnable? Does the commentator make you laugh?
6. **Toss / Gallery**: chain three hits: does the streak feel good? **Crab Chaos**: does "crabs left" make you work together?
7. **Quests**: CLAIM ALL; is the "+N" pop satisfying? **Challenge** each other: is the result clear (scores, tie), and does the rematch ACCEPT card work?
8. **Sanctuary**: ride up: do your pets run to greet you?
9. **Feel**: anything too wordy, cluttered or in the way? Any stutter?

**How to report**: say what felt bad or confusing, and send a screenshot of any red error (View > Output). Numbers are easy to change; name the game and the number.
